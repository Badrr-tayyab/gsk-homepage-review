From a family kitchen. To a shared responsibility.

Founded in early 2024 by brothers Hani and Mahmoud Almadhoun, Gaza Soup Kitchen began in Beit Lahia. The people cooking and serving are part of the communities they support.

HOW IT BEGAN

Four pots. A meal for the neighbours.

Mahmoud and his friends bought large pots, cooking oil and tomato paste, gathered ingredients and cooked for the families around them. Hani helped raise the resources to keep that work going. Mothers, sisters, friends and neighbours helped prepare and serve the food.

A kitchen rooted in its community.

Ingredients are sourced locally whenever possible. Teams adjust to supplies, changing locations and the needs of the people around them.

The work reaches beyond the pot.

Founder updates describe connected forms of support. Availability and scale change with access, safety and resources.

Hot meals

Local teams source ingredients, prepare fresh food and serve people in their own communities.

Drinking water

Water deliveries rotate through communities where clean water is needed.

Hospital meals

Teams bring prepared meals to hospitals to support patients, families and staff.

Food parcels

Parcels reach families through distribution points and direct community assessments.

Learning spaces

Learning activities and school supplies help children stay connected to education.

Medical support

Founder updates describe a local medical point responding to needs on the ground.

CHEF MAHMOUD’S LEGACY

His word remains our promise: Mostamreen.

Mahmoud was killed in November 2024. His family, friends and the local team continue the work. Mostamreen means “we will continue”, a word carried forward in the kitchens and by supporters around the world.

Gaza Soup Kitchen is a grassroots initiative led by the people of Gaza, serving hot meals to thousands daily across 10 kitchen sites.
