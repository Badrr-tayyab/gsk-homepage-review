What is Gaza Soup Kitchen?

Gaza Soup Kitchen is a grassroots Palestine NGO founded in early 2024 by brothers Hani and Mahmoud Almadhoun. Its mission is to provide hot meals and clean water to Palestinians in Gaza facing extreme food insecurity and risk of famine.

Where does Gaza Soup Kitchen operate?

The first kitchen began in Beit Lahia. Local teams work in communities across Gaza, adjusting to displacement, access, supplies and safety. Ingredients are sourced locally whenever possible.

Who runs the work?

Hani Almadhoun, supported by the local team of staff and volunteers in Gaza. Co-founder Mahmoud Almadhoun was killed in November 2024; his family and friends carry on his legacy.

How many people are served?

Numbers change with access, supplies, safety and the location of communities. Updates include figures from particular dates; an older total should not be read as today’s capacity.

How is the work funded?

Private donations support food, water, cooking fuel and essential operations. Use the donation options on this website to reach the selected payment provider directly.

Are donations tax-deductible?

GSK’s supplied organization information identifies a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 99-4047997. Tax treatment depends on the donor’s jurisdiction and circumstances. The payment provider issues the applicable receipt.

Can I give monthly?

Recurring options depend on the provider and campaign. Review the frequency and amount inside the secure checkout before completing your donation.

How do I change or cancel a recurring gift?

Use the donor account or management link from your payment provider. If you need help, contact joinus@gazasoupkitchen.org with the provider name and receipt reference. Never email full card details.

Can I volunteer in Gaza?

GSK does not recruit people from outside Gaza to travel there for physical volunteering. Ask about remote/digital and other suitable roles through Contact.

How else can I help?

Share official posts, use the toolkit’s editable templates, organize a fundraiser, shop or join the free community. See How to Help for direct links.

Where can I read financial information?

The Financial Information page links the FY 2024 Form 990. Financial documents remain separate from the toolkit.

How can I contact GSK?

Email joinus@gazasoupkitchen.org or call +1 (202) 539-7060. Choose a category in the Contact page to help route your inquiry.

Gaza Soup Kitchen is a grassroots initiative led by the people of Gaza, serving hot meals to thousands daily across 10 kitchen sites. Born from a vow to ensure no neighbor goes hungry, our mission continues in honor of co-founder Mahmoud Almadhoun — guided by his word, Mostamreen (“we will continue”).
