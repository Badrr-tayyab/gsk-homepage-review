Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Gaza Soup Kitchen, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with the utmost care and respect. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information about you when you visit our website, make donations, or interact with our services. Your trust is sacred to us. Just as we handle every donation with transparency and accountability, we treat your personal information with the same level of responsibility and care.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with our website or services, you may provide us with: Donation Information:- Name and contact information (email, phone, mailing address)- Payment information (credit card details, billing address)- Donation amount and frequency preferences- Memorial or tribute information for special donations Communication Information:- Email address for newsletters and updates- Messages sent through contact forms- Volunteer application details- Social media interactions and comments Account Information:- Login credentials if you create a donor account- Communication preferences and settings- Donation history and tax receipt preferences

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect: Technical Information:- IP address and general location information- Browser type and version- Device information and operating system- Pages visited and time spent on our site- Referral sources (how you found our website) Cookies and Tracking:- Session cookies for website functionality- Analytics cookies to understand website usage- Preference cookies to remember your settings – Third-party cookies from donation processors and analytics services

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: Mission-Critical Operations – Process donations and send tax-deductible receipts- Distribute funds to our kitchen operations in Gaza- Send impact updates showing how your donations help- Provide donor support and answer your questions Communication and Engagement – Send newsletters with stories from our kitchens- Share urgent appeals when emergency needs arise- Provide tax documentation for your charitable giving- Acknowledge your generosity (with your permission) Website Improvement – Analyze website traffic to improve user experience- Optimize donation processes to make giving easier- Enhance security to protect your information- Customize content based on your interests Legal and Administrative – Comply with legal requirements for charitable organizations- Maintain financial records as required by law- Protect against fraud and unauthorized transactions- Respond to legal requests from authorities when required

How We Share Your Information

We Never Sell Your Information

Gaza Soup Kitchen will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes. Your privacy is not for sale.

Limited Sharing for Operations

We may share your information only in these specific circumstances: Service Providers:- Payment processors to process donations- Email service providers for communications- Analytics services to improve our website- Cloud storage providers to securely store data Legal Requirements:- When required by law, subpoena, or court order- To protect our rights, safety, or property- To prevent fraud or illegal activities- In connection with legal proceedings

Organizational Changes:

– If Gaza Soup Kitchen merges with another organization- During asset transfers (with continued privacy protection)- To successor organizations carrying on our mission With Your Consent:- When you explicitly agree to information sharing- For testimonials or success stories (with your approval)- To recognize major donors (if you choose to be acknowledged)

Data Security

Protecting Your Information

We implement industry standard and comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information including technical and operational safeguards, and payment security features.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

You have important rights regarding your personal information: Access Rights:- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you- Ask about how your information is being used- Receive information in a portable, machine-readable format- Get details about who we’ve shared your information with Correction Rights:- Update or correct inaccurate personal information- Complete incomplete information in our records- Request updates to your communication preferences- Modify your donation preferences or frequency Deletion Rights:- Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)- Unsubscribe from all communications- Close your donor account- Request removal from our mailing lists Communication Choices:- Opt out of non-essential emails while keeping donation receipts- Choose frequency of impact updates and newsletters- Specify preferred communication methods- Update contact information at any time

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:- Email: info@gazasoupkitchen.com- Phone: +1 (202) 539-7060

Third-Party Services

External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Social Media Integration

When you interact with our social media content or share our posts, your actions are subject to the privacy policies of those platforms.

Payment Processors

We use reputable third-party payment processors who have their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

How Long We Keep Information

We retain your personal information – donation records, website data, and deletion of records – only as long as necessary for our legitimate purposes.

Your Continued Use

Your continued use of our website and services after policy changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. By using our website, making donations, or engaging with our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Thank you for trusting Gaza Soup Kitchen with your personal information. Your privacy matters to us as much as your generosity matters to the families we serve.

Gaza Soup Kitchen is a grassroots initiative led by the people of Gaza, serving hot meals to thousands daily across 10 kitchen sites. Born from a vow to ensure no neighbor goes hungry, our mission continues in honor of co-founder Mahmoud Almadhoun — guided by his word, Mostamreen (“we will continue”).
